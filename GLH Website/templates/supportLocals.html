Greenfield Local Hub

Where local producers sell and locals buy

Local food producers

Our goal is to help local food producers by giving them a simple and modern platform to sell their products. GLH provides farmers with the tools they need to reach more customer without losing the community driven spirit of local food. We believe local producers deserve visibility, fair support, and a direct connection to the people who value local food.

Make buying local easier for everyone

We are committed to making it effortless for customers to discover and buy from businesses in their area. Our website brings together a wide range of locally produced foods into one place allowing customers to explore and order from their local food producers

Strengthen community and sustainability

GLH exists to build stronger and more sustainable communities. When people buy local, they support farmers. Our mission is to create a hub that encourages customers to buy food from their local farms and food producers. Together we are creating a more connected future for everyone in our local communities
